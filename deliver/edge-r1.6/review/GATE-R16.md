## Defects / notes for the builder
- F-A (cosmetic, every command except steps): tools/set-runnerlock.sh line 90 `echo "$NEWS"` under `set -u` prints "NEWS: unbound variable" on on/log/off/clear (run continues, DONE printed, nothing wrong written). One-word fix, tested (builder tests 23/23 still, error gone): patch/r16-set-runnerlock-unbound-NEWS.patch (`${NEWS:-}`).
- F-B (record only): a restart that finds the market through the lock level exits at market but writes NO runner_exit row (trade_manage rows ORDER_REJECTED, MARKET_EXIT, LADDER_REPAIR only; log says "after a rejected stop" although the stop was only the wrong side). loop_daily counts it as "no exit row", so X-10 undercounts profit_lock_market. The in-poll path writes the row (F2).
- F-C (pre-existing r1.4, rare): after a kill between entry fill and stop placement the resumed trade has journalId null: the ladder still moved the stop (4 steps seen) but writes no runner_lock rows and the runner_exit row has journalId null, so loop_daily cannot see it.
- Old note: a kill in the ~1.5 s between TP1 fill and the TP2 limit restarts as a live-trail runner (no TP2, no lock).

## What the ladder keeps (study desk numbers, `ideas/runtrail/` engine sim.py, offline estimate, n = 20 NY runners, 1 runner contract, $2/pt; my run: review/edge15-ladder/macladder.py)
Paths are rebuilt by `ideas/runtrail/data.py` into `trades.json` from 1m bars (10/28-11/03) and, for the two 11/11 trades, from waypoints (no 1m bars after 11/06; unseen dips are possible, so 11/11 is a best case).
- 11/11 08:43 long (peak +126.75): today +2 (stop), r1.5 50/10 +10, this ladder +65 (step 3 at +100; step 4 needs +150) = +$126 per runner contract more than today.
- 11/02 09:21 and 09:25 shorts (one move, peak +175/+167, today TP2 +165): CUT. 09:21 exits +110 (adverse-first walk) or +45 (favourable-first walk); 09:25 exits +65. Cost $110-$240 and $200 per contract. 10/30 07:45 -$285 (exit +25), 10/30 09:43 -$169 (exit +110) also cut. 4 of 6 TP2 winners cut.
- Net vs today over the 20 runners: -$496 (opt) / -$626 (pess); vs r1.5 50/10 about -$544 / -$674; saves: 11/11 +126, 10/30 11:44 +86, 11/02 12:10 +46, 10/28 +10. 11/11-sized reversal (+$126) pays for under half of one 11/02 winner cut. Mac accepted this cost; the evidence still says 1 reversal vs 4 cuts in this sample.

## X-10 (office/EXPERIMENTS.md) and loop_daily.py
- X-10 text is r1.5 (one step 50/10, r1.5 install). Rewrite for the ladder: hypothesis "ratchet keeps most of a big runner", cost expected (study: 4 TP2 cuts / 6, -$500 per 20), so the kill clause "cuts a would-be TP2 trade more than once" fires on the first 2 big winners; replace by a ledger: per trade peak, exit, +2-counterfactual (peak and exit are in runner_lock bestPts / runner_exit price), cumulative net vs the +2 stop, keep/kill at 20 NY runners by that net. The "TP2 winners cut" counter (price samples after exit) stays as a lower bound.
- Add a per-lockStep split (exits by lockStep 1-4) and count restart-path market exits (F-B) once the row exists. Ladder text in the card is built from the rows and is fine for 4 steps.

## Apply / rollback
- r1.6 carries the ladder as its default: install it and nothing else; `bash set-runnerlock.sh show` prints the ladder and mode (read only). To pin the same ladder explicitly: `bash set-runnerlock.sh steps "50:25,75:45,100:65,150:110"` (flat, one restart). Config-only on r1.5 (script has no `steps`): add to config-local.json `"sessionRunnerNy": {"lock": {"mode": "on", "steps": [{"armPts":50,"lockPts":25},{"armPts":75,"lockPts":45},{"armPts":100,"lockPts":65},{"armPts":150,"lockPts":110}]}}`, restart flat.
- Rollback to r1.4/r1.5: `bash set-runnerlock.sh clear` first (flat), then roll back; r1.4 refuses any lock line.
