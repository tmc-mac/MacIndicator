LEVELS PRO v2.8: INSTALL (display only; nothing here touches the bot or the account)

v2.8 is TWO indicators. Add both to the same chart.
- Daily Levels PRO v2.8: your levels, lines and tags, as you have them now.
- Levels PRO TIDE v2.8: what the TIDE bot sees at those levels.
They are two scripts because TradingView rejects a single script that large.

1. Daily Levels PRO v2.8 (replaces v2.7.3 on your chart)
   1. Open the Pine Editor. Open your Daily Levels PRO script (v2.7.3).
   2. Select all, delete, paste the whole of LevelsPRO-v2.8.pine, then press "Save".
      Saving over the same script keeps all your settings.
   3. The chart reloads it as "Daily Levels PRO v2.8" (LVL 2.8).
   4. Settings to keep:
      - Everything stays as you had it.
      - The new switch "Old model (r13.x, manage-only)" is under "Old model (v2.8)". Leave it OFF.
      - With it OFF:
        - the tags show the level, its price and SWEPT;
        - the old chip and board, the glow, the wake candles and the 24 old alerts are gone (the bot no longer trades that model);
        - the TIDE board comes from the second indicator.
      - With it ON: v2.7.4 exactly.
      - The "PARITY (retired in v2.8: unused)" group does nothing now. Ignore it.

2. Levels PRO TIDE v2.8 (new)
   1. In the Pine Editor, open a new indicator. Select all, delete, paste the whole of LevelsPRO-TIDE-v2.8.pine.
   2. Press "Save" and name it "Levels PRO TIDE v2.8".
   3. Press "Add to chart".
   4. Settings to keep (the defaults):
      - TIDE layer ON.
      - TIDE history 3000 1m bars. The bot warms on 2000; more means a slower load. If the board says "1H unknown" for a long time, raise it to 5000.
      - Zone bands ON, Plan ON, Blocks / open wait / 12:55 / 13:05 ON.
      - TIDE board ON, Board position "Bottom Left". This keeps it clear of the Levels PRO board, which sits Middle Left by default.
      - Glow ON.
      - Wake candles ON. Levels PRO's own wake candles are off while its Old model is off, so only this script colours the candles.
   5. Chart: any 1m to 60m chart works. The engine always reads the 1m bars inside your candles, the same input the bot uses.
   6. Visual order: right-click the TIDE indicator > "Visual order" > "Send to back", so the level lines and tags of Levels PRO stay on top of the zone bands.

3. How to read it
   - Each TIDE zone is a faint band with its state written at the left.
     Example: "4H Open B · failed▼  ✕ AGAINST_CONTROL"
   - The words are the bot's own:
     - watching / broken / accepted / failed: before a setup;
     - armed: the setup is waiting for its first retest;
     - retest-pending: the retest is in; the 1m rejection close is the trigger;
     - rejected / cancelled / expired: done.
   - ✕ codes say why the bot would not trade it right now (MID_RANGE, AGAINST_CONTROL, TIME_BLOCK, OPEN_WAIT, SESSION_CLOSE, ZONE_USED, RETEST_NOT_FIRST).
   - "R 28400 ctx" = a round number: context only, never a target or a setup.
   - The board:
     - STAND DOWN / WAIT / SET / ARMED;
     - the 4H control (TRAP with its extreme, or BIAS≈);
     - the 1H state;
     - CTRL≈ (the bot's 15m / 30m bias score, recomputed on the chart).
   - "≈" means the chart recomputes it and it may differ slightly from the bot's own read.
   - The chart cannot see the news pause, the early-close days, the bot's grade (A+ trades aim TP2 at the next major pool) or the broker.

4. Alerts (optional)
   On the TIDE indicator: TIDE CONTROL, TIDE SETUP ARMED, TIDE RETEST, TIDE TRIGGER.
   Or choose "Any alert() function call" to get the trigger with entry / stop / TP1 / TP2.
