## 1. Trade rows resolve to their TIDE record (Mac 11/05: "Why aren't the levels traded being reported?")
feat-20924.js, active only while `snapshot.tide` is shadow / live. It builds one record per TIDE signal row (`tide.rows` phase `signal`), merges the `setup_arm` row (zone / kind / level price), the `live_*` rows (order ids, `live_fill` legs entry / tp1 / tp2 / stop with qty and price, `live_result` realizedUsd / fees) and `tide.trades` (orderIds, fills, avgEntry / exitAvgPx / qty, netUsd / realizedUsd, path). Then every broker round trip in `recentTrades` is matched: first on any shared order / position id (the bot's `entryOrderId` / `exitOrderId` / `orderIds` against the record's ids), else on the entry time within ±90 s and the same side; one record per round trip.
- **Now · Recent Trades** (the bot's own `#trades-body` rows): a matched row's size reads the entry-fill qty (the 12:29 trade reads `1x`, the broker list said `3x`) and gets the line `Level 4H Open 28,430.50 · trap · 2 lots · +78.50 pts · +$314.00 fills, before fees · broker net +$309.26 · +5.32R`, the exits line `tp1 1 @ 28,361.75 (04:32) · tp2 1 @ 28,235.75 (06:13)` and a `Why?` that opens the TIDE signal row inline (signal id, setup, time, side, planned entry, stop, TP1, TP2, grade tag with points and names, HTF word, the gates pass / fail, takenWhy or why, and how the row was matched). The old orange `pts UNKNOWN` chip is hidden on a matched row. $ is the bot's realizedUsd / netUsd when sent, else Σ(exit − entry) × qty × dir × $2 − fees (labelled "fills − fees" or "fills, before fees"); when the broker's net for the same round trip differs by more than $1 it is named next to it. R is the per-contract points against |entry fill − planned stop| (else the plan's stopPts); no stop → "R UNKNOWN: no stop on the plan". No entry fill → "qty UNKNOWN: no fill in the journal".
- **Trades · the account box** (`fx23-ar` rows): the same line; a trade already listed under TIDE TRADES (the row with the Show-on-Levels button for the same id) is hidden in the account box, so no trade shows twice. A round trip with no bot record reads, muted, `account fill · not matched to a bot record`; never a bare "Level: not recorded".
- **TIDE trade rows and cards** (Trades, Review: every element with the Show-on-Levels button): the same resolved line in front of the button.
- Witness qc-20924 (fixture `t24-tide1105`: the four 11/05 trades shaped after the live journal — S425-E 01:48 LONG 4H ORH 28,354.75 accepted-break 1 lot, stop at BE; S437-E 03:39 LONG 4H Open 28,430.50 trap 4 lots, tp1 3 @ 28,443.75, stop 1 @ 28,430; S448-E 04:30 SHORT 4H Open 28,430.50 trap 2 lots, fill 28,377.25, tp1 1 @ 28,361.75 (04:32), tp2 1 @ 28,235.75 (06:13), order ids on the fills; the 12:29 SHORT 1 lot @ 26,886.25 with tp2 26,731.50, journal rows only, the broker list saying 3x and +$200.34): M1–M12 pass; on `t23-pnl1105` (six round trips the bot did not place) every row reads "not matched" (N1, N2). The fixture is SYNTHETIC (meta.json says so) because the live journal's `live_fill` row shape was read from the bot's phase list, not from a captured row: the matcher reads `price | px | fillPx | avgPx`, `qty | size | filled | contracts`, `leg | role | which | step` and any `*orderId(s)` / `positionId` field, so a differently named field falls back to the entry-time match, never to a wrong number.
