   certutil -hashfile mobile.html SHA256

      expect: d891ef9d830244f682110662f85f080dd23984cbb29ba1740cb3e34aca4fb815   (unchanged)

2) Upload them and check them on the server:
   scp mobile.html set-terminal.sh root@<HOST>:/root/
   ssh root@<HOST> "sha256sum /root/mobile.html /root/set-terminal.sh"
      (the same two codes as above)

3) Install (it asks "Is the bot FLAT?" - type YES):
   ssh -t root@<HOST> "bash /root/set-terminal.sh install /root/mobile.html d7d7005965364a0b07871717afc9d71be8a9954a65803f923d3bd74795a8f47d"

     DONE: mobile.html installed (sha256 verified): before fc752789 -> after d7d70059.     (you had 2.0.9.23; "before 8b92d047" = you had 2.0.9.22, also fine)

4) On your phone: close the MacMillionz page/app completely and open it again.
   Check the top-right Day P&L: ONE number with "MacMillionz Bot · Day P&L · closed trades only · n trades" under it;
