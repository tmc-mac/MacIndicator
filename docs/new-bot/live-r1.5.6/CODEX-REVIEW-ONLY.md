The experiment preserves strategy configuration while separating observed grade rules from waived eligibility, propagating grade and higher-timeframe provenance, and marking the planned take-one budget exception. The reported planned risk is not actual fill risk or a loss cap.

The driver experiment records dispatch uncertainty, immutable request details and a unique custom tag before broker calls, holds further automatic actions across an ambiguous response or restart, and issues a cancellation at most once per attempt. When the planner records an open position, it now holds before sending a protective-stop cancellation because there is no verified replacement or close path. This avoids deliberately canceling that recorded stop during an attempted change, but does not establish that the stop is actually working or correctly sized. Even an exact terminal-looking cancellation is held because final fill completeness has not been established; normal flatten and stop changes therefore cannot complete. It requires an explicitly successful adapter response before accepting an order ID. The actual `liveOrders` adapter is not present in this overlay, so tag transmission and response compatibility are unverified. The experiment is incomplete and can leave a position without a confirmed working stop. It requires a person to inspect and resolve that state; there is no automated recovery in this experiment. A stop already oversized after a partial fill can still reverse a position. Independent target and full-size stop orders can also both execute before polling and reverse inventory. The experiment detects a same-size opposite-direction position on the tested polling path; this is not a complete reconciliation guarantee. No provider atomic linkage, reduce-only, cancellation, or amendment guarantee has been established. Do not infer live safety from these changes.
